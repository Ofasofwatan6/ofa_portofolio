01

Data Engineering Pipeline for Hotel Booking Analytics

This project developed an end-to-end data engineering pipeline to transform raw hotel booking data into structured, analysis-ready information for business intelligence. The project used the Hotel Booking dataset containing 119,390 reservation records with 36 attributes covering City Hotel and Resort Hotel bookings from July 2015 to August 2017.

ROLE Data Engineer & Data Analyst
TOOLS Pentaho · XAMPP · MySQL · Power BI
DATASET Hotel Booking Demand
DATA 119,390 records · 36 attributes
PERIOD July 2015 – August 2017
OUTPUT MySQL Data Warehouse · Power BI Dashboard

03

Process

The project followed an end-to-end ETL workflow to transform raw hotel booking data into a structured data warehouse for business intelligence analysis.

01
DATA SOURCE

Raw Hotel Booking Data

The Hotel Booking Demand dataset was imported from CSV format. It contains 119,390 reservation records with 36 attributes covering City Hotel and Resort Hotel bookings from July 2015 to August 2017.

02
ETL · PENTAHO

Data Cleansing

The raw data was cleaned and standardized by handling missing values, correcting data types, filtering invalid records, and removing unnecessary attributes before further transformation.

03
ETL · PENTAHO

Data Transformation

The cleaned data was transformed by generating additional attributes such as total nights and estimated revenue. Arrival months were also grouped into seasonal categories for analysis.

04
DATA WAREHOUSE · MYSQL

Star Schema

The transformed data was organized into a MySQL data warehouse using a star schema consisting of one fact table and eleven dimension tables. Surrogate keys were used to maintain relationships between the tables.

05
ETL · PENTAHO

ETL Job Orchestration

A Pentaho ETL Job was used to manage the execution order of the pipeline, starting from raw data loading and cleansing, followed by dimension transformations, and ending with the fact booking load.

06
BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE · POWER BI

Dashboard & Analysis

The resulting data warehouse was connected to Power BI to create an interactive dashboard for analyzing booking cancellations, revenue, seasonality, market segments, distribution channels, and customer characteristics.

04

Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents several analytical views to explore hotel booking performance and identify patterns across booking behavior, revenue, and customer distribution.

01

Executive Summary

The Executive Summary provides an overview of the hotel's overall booking performance through key indicators such as total bookings, total revenue, cancellation rate, and average daily rate. It also allows users to filter the analysis by hotel type, arrival season, and arrival year to compare overall performance across different periods and hotel categories.

02

Cancellation Analysis

The Cancellation Analysis focuses on factors related to booking cancellations, including lead time, deposit type, and previous cancellation behavior. This dashboard helps identify how cancellation rates vary across different booking conditions and customer histories, providing a clearer view of patterns that may affect booking reliability.

03

Revenue Analysis

The Revenue Analysis examines revenue performance across hotel types and arrival months. The dashboard allows the comparison of monthly revenue between City Hotel and Resort Hotel and highlights seasonal patterns that can be observed throughout the analyzed period.

04

Customer Distribution

The Customer Distribution section presents the geographic distribution and characteristics of hotel guests. It helps identify where guests are primarily coming from and provides an overview of customer patterns within the hotel booking dataset, including the dominance of transient customers and the concentration of guests from European countries.

05

Key Insights

The analysis shows that booking cancellations are associated with several booking characteristics. Reservations made more than 180 days in advance showed a 49% cancellation rate in the project analysis, while shorter lead-time bookings showed lower cancellation rates. Deposit type and previous cancellation behavior also showed differences in cancellation patterns. The analysis further found that 89% of reservations had no deposit, providing an important context when examining cancellation behavior.

The dashboard also highlights patterns in revenue, seasonality, and customer distribution. August was identified as the highest revenue month in the project analysis for both City Hotel and Resort Hotel. Online Travel Agencies generated the largest booking volume, while transient customers represented the dominant customer category. The geographic analysis also showed that Europe had the largest concentration of guests within the dataset.
